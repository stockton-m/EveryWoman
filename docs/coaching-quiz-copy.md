## Results

- **Start over**

The tier name (`Foundation`, `Signature`, or `Elevated`) appears under the eyebrow. Each result then shows a “Best for” line and an explanation.

### Foundation

**Best for line:** Best for: self-motivated women who want expert programming and the freedom to run with it.

**Explanation:** Your answers point to a self-directed rhythm: expert programming, monthly progress reviews, and the freedom to train on your own schedule.

### Signature

**Best for line:** Best for: women who want accountability, personalized guidance, and a coach in their corner.

**Explanation:** Your answers favor {phrases} and a coach who can adjust your program as your body and schedule change.

- form feedback
- recovery and lifestyle coaching
- longer-term planning

**Best for line:** Best for: women navigating chronic illness, postpartum recovery, or major life transitions who want close, expert support every step of the way.

**Explanation:** Your current season and support preferences call for {phrases}, with expert guidance that can respond as your needs change.

Up to three of these phrases are inserted, in this order. If none apply, the fallback is used alone.

- care-team coordination
- on-the-fly adjustments
- a close coaching connection
- personalized mobility, breathing, and core work

**Fallback:** high-touch, adaptable support
