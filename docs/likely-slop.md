# Likely slop

Significant on-screen copy as of October 2026. This is a read for passages that sound generated. It is not a comparison against a brief.

A passage is **Review** when it uses an em dash, a semicolon, a contrastive turn (“it’s not X, it’s Y”, “instead of”, “the opposite”), short punchy or stacked parallel sentences, or other LLM cadence (adjective stacks, “X, Y, and Z” closers, a colon followed by a triad, press-release bios). **Clear** means none of that showed up.

No semicolons turned up in this copy.

Included: hero and section intros, body paragraphs, tier intros, quiz result sentences, the full About profile bios (not the truncated cards), the services disclaimer, testimonials, and the contact and 404 intros. Longer than five words.

Left out: posts, nav, buttons, tabs, alt text, metadata, headings and titles (including growth-card headlines and tier taglines), collage captions, benefit-grid feature lines, and quiz question prompts and answer buttons.
